## Overview

A small, hands-on product modification exploring the interface between soft materials and a manufactured object.

## Process

The project focuses on a replacement headband in real leather, with attention to comfort, construction and stitchability. The sewing machine's capability with layered leather is part of the practical constraints. The final replacement is not yet documented as complete.
