## Overview

A personal experiment in bringing the logic of mechanical design into garment construction. The work explores how a traditional shirt pattern can be defined from measurements while retaining consistent seam geometry and practical construction details.

## Process

Initial measurements, a parameter set, prototype geometry and verification notes have been developed. The pattern is still being refined and is not presented as a finished, sewn garment.
