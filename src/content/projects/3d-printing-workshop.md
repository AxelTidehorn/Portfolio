## Overview

A working desktop prototyping setup for small parts, fixtures and experiments. The focus is on useful iterations rather than display pieces.

## Process

After a long period out of use, the Ender 3 V2 was brought back into operation using OctoPrint through Octo4a. The current setup uses a 0.6 mm nozzle and PETG. Trial prints include components for film handling, with fit and tolerance still being evaluated.

This is an ongoing workshop capability, not a claim of a finished product.
