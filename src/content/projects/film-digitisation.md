## Overview

Exploring practical ways of getting photographs from film into a digital workflow, with particular attention to repeatability, alignment, handling and image quality.

## Process

The starting point includes a dedicated 35 mm film scanner and experiments with camera-based reproduction. Film holders and lighting are being evaluated, including 3D-printed test parts. The workflow is still evolving, and there is no claim of a finished custom scanner.

This work also informs [FILMLABB](/projects/filmlabb/), a separate browser-based negative conversion project.
